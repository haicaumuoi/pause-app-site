# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Pause helps you notice and soften the pull of your phone. We built it so that the most personal parts of your day never leave your iPhone. This policy explains what stays on your phone, what reaches our servers, why, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- **Your Screen Time data never leaves your phone.** App names, minutes, pickups and times opened are read and drawn by Apple's Screen Time system inside your iPhone. Apple does not let Pause send them anywhere, and we never see them.
- **Your moods, notes and onboarding answers stay on your phone.** They are stored on your iPhone only. Deleting Pause deletes them.
- **No ads, no selling, no tracking across apps.** We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.
- **An account is optional.** You can use Pause without signing in. If you sign in, we keep only what we need to run your account.
- **You can say no to usage statistics** at any time in Settings › Privacy.

## What stays on your iPhone only

- Screen Time information (apps, websites, categories, minutes, pickups, notifications, times opened), handled by Apple's Family Controls, Device Activity and Managed Settings frameworks.
- The apps you choose as distracting apps and the apps you keep open during Focus.
- Mood check-ins and notes.
- Your onboarding answers (for example your name, age range, goals, wake and bed times).
- Your sigh breaks, walks, focus sessions, streak and garden progress.
- Step counts used during a walk (read from Motion & Fitness on your phone and not stored by us).

## What reaches our servers, and why

We collect the following only when you use the related feature:

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Email address (or Apple's private relay address), the name you give, language, time zone, quiet hours | To create and secure your account and send account emails | Until you delete your account |
| Sign-in data: hashed sign-in codes, session tokens, passkey public keys, Apple or Google account identifiers | To let you sign in safely | Until it expires or you delete your account |
| Device notification token | To deliver reminders you turned on | Until you turn notifications off or delete your account |
| Coarse usage events, such as "completed a sigh break" or "kept a streak" (never Screen Time data, mood, notes or quiz answers) | To understand which features help, if you allow usage statistics | Until you delete your account; you can turn this off at any time |
| Subscription status from Apple | To unlock Premium | While you have Premium |
| A record that the App Store rating prompt was requested (never your rating) | To avoid asking too often | Until you delete your account |
| Support emails you send us | To help you | As long as needed to resolve your request |

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or health records.

## Who helps us run Pause

We use a small number of service providers who process data only on our instructions:

- **Apple** — App Store purchases, Sign in with Apple, notifications.
- **Google** — Sign in with Google, only if you choose it.
- **Our hosting provider** (Railway) — runs our servers and database.
- **Postmark** — sends account emails such as sign-in codes.
- **PostHog** (EU) — product analytics, only if enabled by us and only if you allow usage statistics. It receives a pseudonymous identifier, never your name or email.

We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

## Your choices and rights

- **Usage statistics:** turn "Share usage analytics" on or off in Settings › Privacy.
- **Notifications:** turn reminders off in Settings or in iPhone Settings.
- **Screen Time access:** you can revoke it at any time in iPhone Settings › Screen Time.
- **Export:** you can export your notes from Notes.
- **Delete:** Settings › Delete account removes your account and server data. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone.
- Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or Vietnam), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

## Children

Pause is for people aged 17 and over. Onboarding stops for anyone who says they are younger, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are never stored; sign-in codes and tokens are stored as hashes. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and keep as little as possible.

## Not medical advice

Pause is a wellbeing tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition, including ADHD.

## Changes

If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
