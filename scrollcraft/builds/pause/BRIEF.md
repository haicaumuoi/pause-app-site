# Pause landing page: brief

Interviewed in chat (12 Nov 2026). Answers 1, 2, 7, 8 come from the owner's earlier messages in the same session; 3 to 6 from the interview form.

## Interview
1. **Vibe:** "slow but thoughtful animations", calm. References: blume.codes (phone as the main attraction on the first screen, then a runway through each feature), Mobbin phone landing pages, motionin.design.
2. **Journey:** phone on the first screen → scroll through the features → download.
3. **Energy:** "Calm the whole way".
4. **Feeling and the one moment:** "The page breathes with you": mid-page, a sigh-break orb fills the screen; scrolling is the slow inhale, then the exhale plays on its own.
5. **What no other site does:** the same answer: the page takes a breath with the visitor.
6. **Range:** "Keep the playful sticker look": the app's fonts, paper colours and stickers.
7. **One world or scenes:** distinct scenes (hero phone, chapters, vine runway, breath, close).
8. **Assets:** the app's own stickers (PNG), the app's colour themes, fun-isometric stickers drawn for the page. No photos, no footage. Nothing generated.
Also: "scroll less" → about 8 screens in total (was about 12).

## Feeling curve
- Hero: **welcome**. A big phone, stickers you can throw.
- Chapters: **recognition**. "Oh, that's me opening Instagram." The phone changes screens like the real app.
- Runway: **quiet warmth**. A vine grows through the text and sprouts at each small feature.
- Breath (peak): **stillness**. The page slows to one breath with you.
- Close: **settled**. "Your phone, on your terms", after you've just felt what the app does.

## Peak
"It made me take a breath before I even downloaded it." Lives in the breath act, right before the close, with the longest pinned span on the page. The runway before it is quieter (no pin).

## Tell-someone sentence
It's the site where scrolling down made me breathe in, and the page breathed out for me.

## Authored silence
The exhale: about 6 seconds where the orb shrinks on its own and nothing asks for input. Not dead scroll.
